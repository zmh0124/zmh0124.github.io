🔥 Research Interests

My research spans video anomaly detection and understanding, reliable multimodal intelligence, and RL-based LLM post-training. The roadmap below summarizes my video research.

  • Video Representation Learning. Learning normality and anomalous patterns through multi-scale spatio-temporal modeling, self-supervision, and generative learning.
  • Vision–Language Fine-Grained Understanding. Adapting vision–language models to distinguish anomaly categories and align visual evidence with semantic concepts.
  • Reliable Multimodal Understanding. Diagnosing hallucinations and semantic bias, improving models through preference optimization, and evaluating understanding at multiple semantic levels.
  • RL-based Post-training. Improving mathematical and code reasoning through reinforcement learning, data curricula, and efficient reasoning.
